Adding wall shelves is one of the easiest ways to gain storage and personality without a big renovation or a big budget. The best budget options prove you don’t need to spend much to get clean lines, reliable mounting, and a finished look. When choosing budget shelves, focus on three things: weight capacity, mounting ease, and material quality. Plastic or composite shelves can be very capable for lightweight decor, while solid wood (even at a higher price) adds warmth and durability. Also consider the layout, corner shelves exploit dead space, while floating shelves offer a seamless modern silhouette.
